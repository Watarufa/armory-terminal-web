How To Read The Stats

Stat Legend

  • Damage — base damage per hit to an unarmored target.
  • Fire Rate (RPM) — rounds per minute at full trigger.
  • Magazine — rounds per loaded magazine.
  • Reload (s) — time in seconds for a full reload.
  • Range (m) — effective engagement distance in metres.
  • Recoil (1–10) — kick rating; 1 is a laser, 10 is a bronco.
  • Price ($) — buy-station cost in credits.
