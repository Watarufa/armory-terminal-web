ARMORY HQ

About This Terminal

The ARMORY TERMINAL is the quartermaster's database for the whole buy station. Every weapon that reaches an operator's hands is catalogued here first: logged, stat-tested, and filed under its category. This dossier explains what the terminal is, where the facility sits, and where to read more.

Mission

One place, every loadout. Instead of memorising a wall of numbers, an operator can open a single category page, compare every weapon side by side, and walk out knowing exactly what fits their playstyle and their credits. No hype, no marketing — just stats, roles, and honest trade-offs.

What You Will Find Here

  • Six weapon categories, each on its own page.
  • A full comparison table per category.
  • Lore, combat notes, and unlock requirements for every weapon.
  • Signature showcase footage and external references.

Facility Location

The terminal is housed at a fictional forward armory in the high desert — far from anything worth hitting, close to plenty of open range for stat testing. The map below marks the general training grounds.

Credits & Disclaimer

Build Notes

Armory Terminal is a pure-HTML5 project — no CSS, no JavaScript. The logo and category icons are hand-drawn local SVG. The 24 weapon photos are real-world reference photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons under open licenses (see Image Credits below). All weapon names, stats, and lore are generic and fictional, created for a structure exercise and not tied to any real product, brand, or game.
